🔲 The Computer Engineering Journey

Hello! I am Shaun Steven S. Alejaga, currently navigating my first year as a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering student at Mapúa University. My fascination with technology began at an early age when I wondered how physical electronic components could execute complex logic and algorithms.

Today, my academic pursuits center around understanding the synergy between hardware architecture and software programming. At Mapúa, the rigorous quad-semester system has cultivated in me a strong sense of discipline, efficient time management, and resilience in solving complex computational problems.

Navigating the Mapúa Quad-Semester System

A freshman's perspective on grit, growth, and engineering passion.

Stepping into Mapúa University as a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering student was both exhilarating and daunting. Known for its rigorous quad-semester academic calendar, Mapúa tests not only your cognitive abilities in calculus and physics but also your absolute discipline and time management.

Overcoming Initial Academic Challenges

During my first major programming and calculus assessments, I faced the shock of fast-paced academic delivery. Instead of getting overwhelmed, I adapted by forming study groups, leveraging faculty consultations, and dedicating consistent hours to hands-on coding practice. This challenge taught me that engineering is less about knowing all the answers immediately and more about developing a systematic approach to problem-solving.

Every circuit schematic analyzed, every debugging session in C++, and every collaborative group project has shaped my technical and personal growth. I have learned to embrace constructive failure as a vital step toward innovation.

Looking ahead, I am excited to delve deeper into microprocessors, embedded systems, and advanced hardware design. My journey at Mapúa is just beginning, and I am fully committed to growing into a competent engineer ready to tackle global technological challenges.
